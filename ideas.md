# Ideas

## Idea 1 – Dental clinic appointment booking system (real client, going live)

- **Pitch:** Patients book a 30-minute slot online with OTP and see how busy each slot is; reception manages the day from one fast screen instead of a paper diary.
- **Business:** Single-dentist dental clinics (first client: a clinic in Jammu).
- **How they handle it today:** Phone calls, walk-ins, and a paper appointment diary; reminders by phone, if at all.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** The Jammu clinic's dentist and receptionist, plus 1 dental clinic in Ludhiana to test if other clinics want this.

## Idea 2 – Hosiery pending-order & dispatch tracker

- **Pitch:** Paste a WhatsApp order and it becomes a tracked order, so you always know what's pending for each dealer.
- **Business:** Hosiery wholesalers and small knitwear manufacturers selling to dealers.
- **How they handle it today:** WhatsApp chats, phone calls, paper registers, Excel, staff memory. Billing in Tally/Busy, but pending and partial dispatches are hard to track.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** 2 hosiery wholesalers in the Ludhiana wholesale market.

## Idea 3 – Bakery pre-order & capacity planner

- **Pitch:** One order link for custom cakes, plus daily slot limits so a shop never overbooks a peak day.
- **Business:** Cake shops and bakeries (home bakers as a secondary market).
- **How they handle it today:** WhatsApp conversations, calendar reminders, handwritten lists.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** 1 cake shop near my college.
