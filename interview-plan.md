Go during quiet hours; keep each visit under 15 minutes.

## Questions

1. Walk me through how an order or appointment reaches you, from the first call or message to the end.
2. Tell me about the last time something went wrong with it. What happened?
3. How did you sort it out? How long did it take?
4. How do you know today what's pending or who's coming?
5. Who handles this? Is it you or your staff?
6. What have you already tried to fix it?
7. What did that cost you, in money or customers?
8. Who else should I talk to about this?

### Extra for clinics

- How many patients don't show up in a typical week?
- What happens when too many walk in at once?

### Questions from rehearsal (dentist)

> "I'm a student studying how clinics handle the evening rush. I'm not selling anything today. Could I ask you three quick questions?"

1. Can you walk me through yesterday evening, from the first patient to the last?
2. Tell me about the last time the waiting room was full. What happened?
3. How many patients left without being seen last week? Do they come back?
4. How many calls a day ask "is there a rush?" Who handles them?
5. Have you tried any app or software for appointments? What happened with it?
6. What happens on days your receptionist is on leave?
7. How many booked patients don't turn up in a typical week? What do you do about it?
8. Who else should I talk to, maybe your receptionist?
