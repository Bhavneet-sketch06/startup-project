Go during quiet hours; keep each visit under 15 minutes.

## Questions

1. Walk me through how an order or appointment reaches you, from the first call or message to the end.
2. Tell me about the last time something went wrong with it. What happened?
3. How did you sort it out? How long did it take?
4. How do you know today what's pending or who's coming?
5. Who handles this? Is it you or your staff?
6. What have you already tried to fix it?
7. What did that cost you, in money or customers?
8. Who else should I talk to about this?

### Extra for clinics

- How many patients don't show up in a typical week?
- What happens when too many walk in at once?
